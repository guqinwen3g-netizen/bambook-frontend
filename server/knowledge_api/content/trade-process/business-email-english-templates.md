# 外贸函电英文模板（Business Email English Templates）

高频场景的标准英文邮件框架。模板是骨架，**个性化细节（客户名/订单号/具体诉求）才是转化关键**——群发感强的模板邮件回复率极低。

## 一、询盘回复（Inquiry Reply）

```
Subject: Re: Inquiry for Cotton T-shirts - [Company Name]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your inquiry dated [date] regarding [product].

We are pleased to offer the following:
- Item: 100% combed cotton T-shirt, 180gsm
- Price: USD 2.85/pc FOB Shanghai (based on 5,000 pcs)
- Sampling: 7 days | Bulk lead time: 35 days after PP sample approval
- Payment: 30% T/T deposit, balance against B/L copy

Attached please find our detailed quotation and product photos.
Could you confirm the size breakdown and any specific testing requirements?

We look forward to your feedback.

Best regards,
[Name] | [Title]
[Company] | [WhatsApp] | [Website]
```

**要点**：24 小时内回复（首因效应）；先答后问（给方案再提澄清问题）；附 2-3 个引导性问题推进对话。

## 二、报价跟进（Quotation Follow-up）

```
Subject: Following up: Quotation #[No.] - [Product]

Dear [Name],

I hope this email finds you well. I'm following up on our quotation 
sent on [date] for [product].

To recap: [one-line offer summary].

May I ask:
1. Does the pricing align with your target?
2. Is there any adjustment needed on specs or quantity?

We're holding fabric stock for this program until [date] - 
happy to arrange samples within 5 days if helpful.

Best regards,
```

**节奏**：报价后 3 天首跟、7 天二跟（带新信息：如面料行情/交期提示）、14 天末跟（轻推+留门）。

## 三、确认订单（Order Confirmation）

```
Subject: Order Confirmation - PO #[No.]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your purchase order #[No.]. Please review the following 
confirmation:

- Style: [款号] | Qty: [数量] | Unit price: USD [x] FOB [port]
- Delivery: [date] | Payment: [terms]
- PP sample: to be submitted by [date]

Kindly confirm by return email. We will proceed with fabric booking 
upon your confirmation and deposit receipt.

Best regards,
```

## 四、生产进度通报（Production Update）

```
Subject: Production Update - PO #[No.] - On Schedule

Dear [Name],

Quick update on your order:
- Fabric: arrived factory on [date], inspection passed
- Cutting: completed [date]
- Sewing: 40% complete, on track for [date] completion
- Inspection: booked with [SGS/ITS] for [date]

No issues to report. We'll share the inspection report once available.

Best regards,
```

**原则**：主动通报（不等客户问）；坏消息比好消息更要早报（延误预警提前 7 天+）。

## 五、延误通知（Delay Notification）

```
Subject: Important: Delivery Update - PO #[No.]

Dear [Name],

I'm writing to inform you of a delivery adjustment on PO #[No.].

Due to [原因：fabric dyeing lot delay / typhoon port congestion], 
the ex-factory date will move from [原日期] to [新日期] (a [x]-day delay).

Mitigation plan:
- [方案1：分批出货 - first 2,000 pcs by air on [date] at our cost]
- [方案2：后续海运恢复原计划]

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. The revised schedule 
is confirmed with the factory in writing (attached).

Best regards,
```

**要点**：先说结论 → 说原因（客观）→ 给方案（不是只道歉）→ 担责行动（费用承担明确）。

## 六、催款（Payment Reminder）

```
Subject: Friendly Reminder: Payment for Invoice #[No.]

Dear [Name],

I hope everything is going well with the [product] shipment 
(B/L #[No.], ETA [date]).

This is a friendly reminder that payment of USD [amount] under 
Invoice #[No.] is due on [date]. 

Could you kindly advise the payment schedule? If there are any 
issues with the documents, please let us know immediately.

Best regards,
```

**阶梯**：到期前 3 天友好提醒 → 逾期 3 天正式催款 → 逾期 14 天升级（提及信用额度冻结/中信保）。

## 七、客诉回复（Claim Response）

```
Subject: Re: Quality Issue - PO #[No.] - Our Action Plan

Dear [Name],

Thank you for bringing this to our attention, and we sincerely 
apologize for the [问题] found in PO #[No.].

We take this very seriously:
1. Our QC team is reviewing the [photos/inspection report] you provided
2. We have quarantined the remaining stock for re-inspection
3. We will provide our analysis and resolution proposal within 48 hours

Your patience is appreciated. We are committed to resolving this 
to your satisfaction.

Best regards,
```

**纪律**：48 小时内响应；先承认问题存在（≠承认全责）；给时间表不给空头支票；解决方案书面化（补货/折扣/退款比例）。

## 八、通用规范

- **Subject**：Re:/[Action]: + 关键信息（订单号/品名）——可检索性；
- **长度**：一屏内（超过 150 词拆 bullet）；
- **时态**：确认事实用现在时，承诺动作用将来时 + 日期（"will send by Friday, Aug 14"）；
- **避免**：Chinglish 直译（"please kindly"）、全大写、模糊词（"ASAP"→ 给具体日期）；
- **签名档**：姓名/职位/公司/WhatsApp/网站——方便客户转渠道联系。
